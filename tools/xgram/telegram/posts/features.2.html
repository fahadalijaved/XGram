⭐ What XGram can do · 2/4 🧵 Forum topics, done properly • Topic list: sorted and live, with search, "Mark all topics as read", and pinned topics you can reorder by dragging • Tabs: "All" plus a tab per topic, along the top or as a side strip (remembered per chat); tap to switch in place, long-press for the topic menu • View as messages: a pill with the topic name starts each run of messages, and "View in topic" opens a message inside its topic • Create, rename, pin, close, delete and mute topics (1 h, 8 h, 2 days or forever); pick one of Telegram's topic icons and, for new topics, a color • Each topic has its own unread, mention and reaction badges, typing status, pinned messages, drafts and read ticks • An @ button jumps to the next unread mention • A topic profile with the topic's own media, link and notification settings • Topic links open the right message, sharing and forwarding can target a topic, and notifications read "Sender › Topic" and open inside it • The chat list shows "Topic › Sender: text", with the badge summed over all topics • Pull up at the end of a topic for the next unread topic • Group owners pick the layout in the group's settings → Topics: Off, Tabs or List 👥 Communities • "Added to the X community" previews in chat and topic lists • Tap that message, or ⋮ → Open community, to see the community's chats, the ones you're in first • Optional: one chat list row per community, with its summed unread count; tap to open it, hold to preview

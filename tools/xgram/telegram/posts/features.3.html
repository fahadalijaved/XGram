⭐ What XGram can do · 3/4 💬 Chats and messages • Sponsored messages hidden (on by default) • Message menu: Save to Saved Messages, Repeat (+1), Edit history, Messages by this sender, Hide messages from this person, Bookmark, Reply privately, Copy photo, Clear from cache and Details. You choose which options a long press shows • Details: IDs, exact dates, forward origin, file info, and when your message was read in private chats; tap to copy • Tap a message's time for the exact sent and edited dates with seconds (option), and show seconds in message times (option) • Chat menu: Rename locally (the new name shows everywhere), Go to first message, Delete my messages, Export chat as HTML or text, and Recent actions for supergroup admins • Message filters with words or regex, and shadow ban; optionally hide messages from blocked users in groups • Bookmarks, kept on your device, and Saved by chat for Saved Messages • Local last seen: for people who hide theirs, the last time XGram saw them online, typing or sending • Fixed link previews for X, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, Bluesky and Pixiv posts you send, while the text keeps the original link (on by default) • Long-press bot buttons to copy their text, link or callback data • Pull up at the end of a channel for the next unread channel (on by default) • Checklists, stories, gifts, paid media, invoices and group calls show as text instead of "unsupported message" • Options: "Delete for everyone" ticked by default, forward without author by default, hide the floating new chat button 🛡️ Privacy • Tracking parameters (utm_*, fbclid, si, …) are removed from links you open (on by default) • Hide your own phone number and block Android content capture • Proxy rules: turn the proxy off on VPN, Wi-Fi or mobile data; prefer IPv6 • Unblock everyone at once, or only deleted accounts

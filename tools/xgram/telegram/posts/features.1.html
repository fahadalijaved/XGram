⭐ What XGram can do · 1/4 XGram keeps Telegram X's fast, smooth interface and adds the features below, many of them inspired by AyuGram, NagramX and exteraGram. Most options live in Settings → XGram, and every page there has a search button. 👻 Ghost mode Settings → XGram → Ghost mode. A switch in the side menu turns it all on or off, an indicator in the header shows when it's on, and there's a Quick Settings tile too. Turn on only what you want: • Don't read messages: open chats without marking them read • Hide "listened" on voice and video messages • Don't send typing status, or show "typing" instead of "choosing a sticker" • Stay offline • Mark the chat read when you send, react or vote in a poll • Send as scheduled while offline (opt-in) and send without sound • Per chat: ⋮ → "Ghost mode: read normally here" • Per message: "Read up to here" marks the chat or topic read when you decide Chats you read in ghost mode can look read on this device while they stay unread on the server. Channels are read normally, since they have no read receipts. 🗂️ Message history Settings → XGram → Message history • Deleted messages stay in the chat, marked with a trash icon next to the time • Edited messages keep their earlier versions: message menu → Edit history • Formatting is kept, and media of deleted messages that you had downloaded can be saved too • A viewer lists every chat with deleted or edited messages, with all versions • Keep history for a number of days (30 by default) or forever; open a chat's history from its ⋮ menu History covers messages you receive. Your own deletions and histories you clear aren't kept.
